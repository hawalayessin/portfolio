# Business Intelligence projects

Two academic Power BI projects are already available in this portfolio. Their
reports are downloadable; screenshots allow review without Power BI Desktop.

## FIFA World Cup — 1930–2014

- [Dashboard preview](../Portfolio/public/projects/fifa-world-cup/dashboard.png)
- [Power BI report](../Portfolio/public/projects/fifa-world-cup/fifa-world-cup.pbix)
- [Existing project report](../Portfolio/public/projects/fifa-world-cup/rapport.pdf)

Scope: CSV preparation with Python/Pandas, deduplication and joins, SQLite star
schema, then visual analysis of matches, goals and attendance. The public assets
do not include the complete preparation scripts or an exported DAX catalog.
Those cannot be reconstructed reliably from screenshots alone. Add the original
scripts and exported measures when available; keep source attribution and data
redistribution rights with them.

## Car Sales

- [Dashboard preview](../Portfolio/public/projects/car-sales/dashboard.png)
- [Power BI report](../Portfolio/public/projects/car-sales/car-sales.pbix)

Scope: Excel-based dashboard showing year-to-date sales, average vehicle price
and vehicles sold, with product and geographic breakdowns. The original Excel
source and redistribution license are not documented in the repository. Confirm
them before publishing a dataset. Do not substitute invented sales conclusions
or DAX formulas for the actual report.

## Reviewing a report

Open the `.pbix` locally in Power BI Desktop, inspect relationships and measures,
and configure any missing local source paths before refreshing. A screenshot is
a preview, not proof that refresh works in a clean environment. Use only data
that can lawfully be shared in public demonstrations.
